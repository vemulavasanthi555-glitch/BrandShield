ABOUT BRANDSHIELD

Protecting Your Brand in the Digital World.

BrandShield is a digital risk protection platform designed to help businesses identify and manage potential online threats to their brands.

🛡️

Our Mission

Our mission is to help businesses protect their brand identity, detect suspicious online activity, and manage digital risks through a simple and user-friendly platform.

What BrandShield Offers

  • Website Monitoring: Identify potentially suspicious websites.
  • Social Media Monitoring: Review possible brand impersonation.
  • Mobile App Monitoring: Assess potentially suspicious applications.
  • Risk Scanner: Evaluate suspicious listings and potential threats.
  • Reports: Review and manage investigation results.

Our Goal

We aim to make digital brand protection easier by bringing monitoring, risk assessment, and investigation management together in one place.
